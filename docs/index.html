Timesheeps

The sheep remembers so you don't have to.

A quiet app for Windows that watches what you work on, so filling out your timesheet doesn't require guessing.

Download latest release

What the sheep does

  • Automatic window tracking — records the active app and window title in the background
  • Visual daily timeline — colour-coded blocks show exactly where your time went, hour by hour
  • Drag-to-create time entries — draw blocks on the timeline and assign them to projects
  • Idle detection — notices when you step away and prompts you on return
  • Noise filtering — ignores short windows, merges gaps, and hides apps that don't matter
  • Stays out of the way — lives in the system tray, everything stored locally, no accounts or cloud sync
